## Original study
Yan Wang, Xiaojiang Liu, Shuming Shi (Tencent AI Lab). **Deep Neural Solver for Math Word Problems**. EMNLP 2017, pp.845–854. https://aclanthology.org/D17-1088/ . Formal PDF/metadata preserved in evidence/paper.pdf and paper-meta.html.
The study crawled more than 60,000 elementary Chinese problems from online education sites; rule-based extraction of equations and structured answers produced 23,161 one-unknown-variable linear problems. Table3: 2,187 equation templates,70.1k sentences,822k words. Number mapping and significant number identification (SNI) are original method components. Table5 fivefold average: ZDC42.1; retrieval46.0/47.2; seq2seq53.7/58.1; hybrid61.1/64.7 (without/with SNI). Table7 is a separate nonoverlapping-template split and must not replace Table5. Model results are quotations, not new training or scoring.
