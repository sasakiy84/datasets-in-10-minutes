# MultiChallenge attribution

MultiChallenge: A Realistic Multi-Turn Conversation Evaluation Benchmark Challenging to Frontier LLMs.

Ved Sirdeshmukh, Kaustubh Deshpande, Johannes Mols, Lifeng Jin, Ed-Yeremai Cardona, Dean Lee, Jeremy Kritz, Willow Primack, Summer Yue, Chen Xing. Scale AI, 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.17399

Current data: Scale AI, MultiChallenge, test, revision 670fd81f270314d0f8f82b34fcfe2187fd28f699. https://huggingface.co/datasets/ScaleAI/MultiChallenge

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Thirteen distinct conversations are quoted from the current public dataset. Original wording is retained; blank lines can be compacted in the slide typography. Japanese translations and commentary were added by the slide authors. Fixed conversation histories include existing model responses and are presented as benchmark inputs, not as independent factual advice or newly generated benchmark solutions.

The official-site screenshot illustrates the public Scale Labs MultiChallenge page and leaderboard, cited on the slide. It is not distributed under the dataset license.
