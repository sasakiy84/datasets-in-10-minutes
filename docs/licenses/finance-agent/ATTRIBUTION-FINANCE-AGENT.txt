Source: Vals AI; Bigeard et al. (2025), Finance Agent Benchmark: Benchmarking LLMs on Real-world Financial Research Tasks, https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.00828

This deck by sasakiy84 (2026-10-22) reproduces 13 selected complete public benchmark questions and provides Japanese translations of their questions/reference answers, native tables, and explanatory calculations. These translations and presentation changes are ours, not an endorsement by the benchmark authors. Question text, reference answers and rubric originals are retained separately in examples.json and raw/data--public.csv. The CC BY 4.0 scope here is the original public 50; no assertion is made about licensing of the newer public 27.

Corporate filings and official benchmark website screenshots are separately attributed to their publishers in the deck and sources.md; their copyright is not relicensed by the dataset or software license. The deck presents selected financial facts and short supporting material for explanation; corporate filings are not reproduced in full.
